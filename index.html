This example is based on a real dispatch spreadsheet workflow. The public preview is sanitized, but the structure is real: a control panel that imports new orders, syncs report data, updates statuses, refreshes stock views, and generates handoff lists from one place.

01 Import the new work

Pull in fresh order or report data without hunting through tabs by hand.

02 Clean and match records

Normalize messy files, match keys, skip conflicts, and keep the workbook trustworthy.

03 Update the daily view

Refresh statuses, dates, stock views, and exception lists from one workflow.

04 Export the handoff

Generate the list, summary, email draft, or next action your team actually needs.
